GIRNet: Graph-Based Interference Reduction

Rajesh R.
University of Illinois Chicago

GitHub | [PDF]

Abstract

We present GIRNet, a neural network for interference reduction in multitrack music recordings. It models multiple channels as nodes in a graph and applies a GAT bottleneck to produce interference-reduced signals from raw waveforms. GIRNet outperforms prior methods in terms of SDR and runtime efficiency, and generalizes well to real-world recordings.
