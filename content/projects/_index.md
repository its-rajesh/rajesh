<div class="projects-connection">
  <span>How they connect</span>
  <p><strong>All four projects ask how much structure an audio system really needs.</strong> Interference reduction exploits relationships among microphones; distance-aware separation exploits scene geometry; sensor networks exploit information distributed across space; and the DRC project asks whether explicit separation is always required before downstream processing.</p>
